Featured Project

Madden League Management Website

A web-based tool designed to help Madden players manage weekly league schedules and track statistics.

Open Live Project

The Problem

Madden leagues that are organized through Discord can become difficult to manage as the number of players and weekly games increases. Important information can be spread across Discord messages, making it harder for players to know when games are scheduled or how their statistics compare with other players.

The Solution

My project creates a central website where league members can find their weekly schedule and view important league statistics. Instead of searching through Discord messages, players can use the website to find the information they need.

Project Features

  • Weekly Madden game scheduling
  • League statistics tracking
  • Player-focused information
  • Responsive design for phones and computers
  • Simple navigation for Discord communities
