About Me

My web development experience comes from working with Madden communities on Discord. I noticed that league members needed easier ways to keep up with game schedules and weekly results, so I began creating websites that organize this information in one place.

My goal is to build simple, useful websites that solve real problems for online gaming communities. I enjoy working with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to turn an idea into something that people can actually use.

What I Build

League websites that organize schedules, standings, player information, and weekly statistics.

Who I Build For

Madden players and Discord league communities that need a central place to manage their seasons.

My Approach

I focus on clean layouts, readable information, responsive design, and features that are easy to use.
